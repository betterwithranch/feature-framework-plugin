# 0068. What is missing is asked within its limits

Status: accepted, 2026-11-04

## Context

[Decision 0027](0027-complete-requirements-and-verification.md) said anything missing from a requirement or its verification is asked of the user, one question at a time, unless the work can find it out. Its absolute "anything" had exceptions elsewhere: a question for a later level waits for that level, the work's own decisions aren't asked of the user, and what the work can find out isn't the user's to settle. The user was asked (FF-44): "Reword the "anything missing is asked" requirement to name its limits?", with the proposal "Anything missing from either that is yours to settle at this level is asked of you, one question at a time". The user answered: "ff-44 a. this decision was already made. absolute terms are conflicts with exclusions. they can't co-exist".

## Decision

Anything missing from a requirement or its verification that is the user's to settle at this level is asked of the user, one question at a time.

## Consequences

- Amends [decision 0027](0027-complete-requirements-and-verification.md).
- It is the source of the Outcome's R15.
