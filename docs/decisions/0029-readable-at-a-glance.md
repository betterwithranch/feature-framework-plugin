# Readable at a glance

Every document and message meant for a person follows these rules:

- One decision per question.
- One question at a time; the next waits for the answer.
- A question gives the reader enough to answer it without asking back: a concrete example of each option in the project's own terms, and what each costs. Process notes aren't supporting detail.
- A short title.
- At most two short background sentences.
- Two or three one-line options.
- Short bullets under `## ` subheads, with a blank line around each subhead.
- No walls of text.

The framework carries its own copy of these rules; where it keeps them is decided at Behaviour and Design.
