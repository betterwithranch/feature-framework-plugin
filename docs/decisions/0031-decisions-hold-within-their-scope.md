# A decision holds within its scope

- A decision applies only within its stated scope. "All files use streaming" covers a second file; "file 1 uses streaming" doesn't.
- The work's decision in one feature is guidance, a preferred default, for the same decision in a later feature. That decision is made again and recorded again, citing the earlier one.
- An agreement or codified rule whose scope already covers the later case leaves nothing to decide.
