# 0035. What a level's review checks

Status: accepted, 2026-11-04

## Context

The user said "reviews aren't supposed to be reviewing for defects. they are supposed to be reviewing that the level matches the standards to complete the level and that the artifact is consistent and complete."

## Decision

A level's review checks that the level's document meets the standards for completing that level and is consistent and complete. It does not hunt for defects. A level passes its review when its document does.
