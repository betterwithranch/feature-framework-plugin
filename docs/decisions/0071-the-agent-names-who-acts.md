# 0071. "The agent" names who acts

Status: accepted, 2026-11-10

## Context

The documents and review rules used "the work" for who asks, plays back, decides and records, and the glossary didn't define it. Asked whether to add a glossary entry for "the work" (FF-71), the user chose B: no entry, and the term is replaced everywhere. Asked what replaces it (FF-72), the user chose B, "the agent", because "this needs to work when run with codex too and naming it claude will break that".

## Decision

- "The agent" replaces "the work" wherever it names who acts or decides, and "the agent's" replaces "the work's".
- The framework runs with Codex as well as Claude, so its wording names no particular assistant.

## Consequences

- Applies to the Outcome, the glossary, the review rules, the Outcome definition, the decision classification and the write and review skills.
- Earlier records keep their quotes of past wording.
