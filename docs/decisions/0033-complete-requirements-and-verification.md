# Complete requirements and verification

- A requirement is complete when its verification can be written in full and run, with nothing left to ask the user.
- A verification is complete when it names what is checked, how, and what result passes.
- Anything missing from either is asked, one question at a time, unless the work can find it out.
- A level's document speaks of requirements, how they're verified, and "a level". The Outcome names no lower level, nothing a lower level holds, and no found facts per level.
